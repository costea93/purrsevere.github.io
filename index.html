Purrsevere

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Purrsevere is a free bodyweight workout app. In this release, your workout information is kept on your device. The app does not offer accounts or a Purrsevere cloud backup.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the workouts you record, exercise sets, plans, goals, history, and preferences in local app storage so these features work offline. This information is not uploaded to a Purrsevere server in the current release. The app does not ask you to create an account, and its friends, challenges, and cloud sync features are not available in this release.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can schedule workout reminders and workout activity alerts on your device. Notification permission is optional. You can change it in your device settings. The current release does not register your device for Purrsevere remote push notifications.

Analytics, advertising, and purchases

The current release does not use a product analytics service, display ads, or offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not sell your workout information.

Contacting us

If you choose to email purrsevere.app@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use it to respond to and handle your request. Your email provider and Google, which hosts our support mailbox, also process that email under their own policies. We retain support correspondence while it is needed for support or recordkeeping, and delete it on request unless a legal obligation requires retention. You can request deletion by emailing the same address.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts or advertising to it. GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses and other request information to operate and secure Pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Keeping and deleting app data

Workout information remains in the app's local storage until you delete it or remove the app and its data. Purrsevere does not have a server copy to restore it from. Your phone's own backup service may retain app data according to your device and backup settings; manage those copies through your operating-system account. If you lose your device or remove the app without a usable device backup, your progress may be lost.

Security

App data is kept in the device's app storage and is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your operating system up to date. Emails you send to us are handled by email providers rather than by the app.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if the app's data practices change. Features such as accounts, cloud sync, or new analytics would require an updated policy before they are released.

Questions

Purrsevere is the app covered by this policy. For privacy questions or requests, contact its developer at purrsevere.app@gmail.com.
